Privacy policy

The openclaw-career-assistant Google app is a personal tool. Only I use it, on my own computer, with my own Google account.

What it accesses. My Google Calendar events, my Google Tasks, and one Google Sheet (read only), so they show on my desktop and stay in sync.

Where the data goes. Nowhere else. It stays on my computer and in my Google account. It isn't shared, sold, or used for ads or anything else, and no one else's data is accessed.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
